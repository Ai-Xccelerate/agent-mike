# Custom work and chargeable help

Some requests go beyond standard support. Use **Professional Services** for work such as data manipulation, SQL queries, or creating or changing a report. Customization requests, including new features, are handled by the team and may be chargeable.

- The support team can tell you what is possible and what it would involve. Your account manager owns final pricing and special arrangements.
- Not every feature request is built or scheduled. Some may be deferred or not completed.
- If your email or accounts are hosted on your own servers, your own IT team looks after them. EAP Expert's help in your own environment is typically billable.

If you are not sure whether something is standard support or chargeable work, raise it as a General Question and the team will confirm.
