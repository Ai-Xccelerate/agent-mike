# Screen sharing with the support team

For many problems it is quicker if a member of the support team can see what you see. The team may offer a short screen share on Microsoft Teams. The session can be recorded so the steps to repeat the problem, and the build date of your product, are captured.

To arrange it, the team needs your time zone and a time that suits you, so the meeting is set for a time that works where you are. A member of the team sends the meeting request.

It helps to have ready:
- the product and screen where the problem happens;
- the exact error message, if there is one;
- the build date of your EAP Expert product, if you know it, and whether it runs on your own computer or server or on one of EAP Expert's hosted systems.

Please make sure nothing on your screen shows client or patient information during the session.
