# What to include in a support request

Every request needs a short **summary**, a **description** of what you need or what happened, and who the request is raised on behalf of.

Some request types ask for more:

- **Report a Bug**: choose a **priority** (this is required for bugs) and, if it helps, attach a file such as a screenshot. Say what you were doing just before the error and the exact error message.
- **Request a New Feature** and **Suggest Improvement**: these ask for the **product**, the **detail** of what you want, and the **new fields** involved. You can attach a file.
- **General Questions**: you can add the **build date** of your EAP Expert product if you know it, and attach a file.
- **User / License Change**, **Training Request**, **System Unavailable**, **Professional Services** and **Security / Compliance Questions**: a summary and a description are enough. These types do not take an attachment.

Clear details help the team answer in one go. If the team needs more, they will ask specific questions.
