# How fixes and updates reach you

When the support team has a change for your request, it is tested in a test environment (User Acceptance Testing) before it reaches you, and the change is reviewed by a second programmer.

- The team tells you when and where a tested change is ready for you to try, and asks you to confirm that it works for you. A request is not described as fixed until you have tried it.
- If your system is hosted by EAP Expert, your account manager coordinates a time for the update with you. The update is made at the time agreed.
- If your system runs on your own servers, you receive update instructions.
- Changes that matter to customers come with release notes in plain language.

If the change doesn't work for you, tell the team on the same request and they will look at it again.
