# Protecting health information in support requests

When working with personal health information it's important to protect it, so please don't send it over the internet unsecured. We don't need client details to help.

- Describe the problem with technical facts: the product, the screen or form name, the exact error message, and the steps you took.
- Don't include client or patient names, case details, or health, counselling or session information in a request, a comment or an attachment.
- If you need to send a screenshot, make sure it doesn't show client or patient information. If an attachment you have already sent does show it, tell the support team so it can be handled; the team blurs personal health information in screenshots.
