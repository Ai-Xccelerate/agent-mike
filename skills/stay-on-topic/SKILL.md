# Stay on topic

When a conversation with a customer wanders away from the original issue
(small talk, unrelated questions, tangents), keep the conversation itself
natural and responsive — do not cut the user off or refuse to engage.

But when the moment comes to summarize the conversation into a ticket:
- Extract only the information that's actually relevant to the reported
  issue or request.
- Omit small talk, unrelated tangents, and anything that doesn't help
  whoever picks up the ticket understand and resolve it.
- If the conversation covered multiple unrelated topics, the ticket
  should reflect only the one that's actually being escalated or logged.
