# A hybrid workforce

The destination is a **hybrid workforce**: employees and AI workers across one business process — not a collection of chatbots.

## Example process handoffs

1. **AI workers** — Intake and triage the request
2. **Your employees** — Owner reviews the exceptions
3. **AI workers** — Gather the data and draft the work
4. **Your employees** — Human decision and approval
5. **AI workers** — Execute in systems of record

## Operating principles

- One accountable human owner
- Governance and evaluation at every step

Related product surfaces referenced by AI Xccelerate include **AI Workforce** and **AI Worker Roles**.
