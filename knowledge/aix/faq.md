# Frequently asked questions

## What does AI Xccelerate do?

We help enterprises move AI from pilots to production — identify valuable opportunities, prove them with working systems, and deploy under the customer's governance.

## Are you selling another AI platform?

No. Positioning is as an **execution partner**, not a platform demo. The first conversation is about the business problem and the next 12 months of change.

## What do we get from a first engagement?

An AI roadmap and a working **Proof of Impact**.

## Do we have to use your infrastructure?

No. We adapt to the customer's cloud and models. We may bring accelerators such as Parchment, Agent Mem, Agent DB, Skills, Scribe, or AIX Core when they speed delivery.

## Which clouds and models do you support?

Clouds include Azure, AWS, Google Cloud, Cloudflare, Vercel, and Railway. Models include OpenAI, Anthropic, Microsoft Copilot, and open source.

## How do I contact AI Xccelerate?

Use **Talk to Us** on [aixccelerate.com](https://www.aixccelerate.com/). Escalate commercial or scoping requests to the human manager when the visitor wants a proposal or pricing.
