# How an engagement runs

AI Xccelerate engagements follow: **Discover → Prioritize → Prove → Deploy → Scale**.

## First engagement stages

1. **Discover** — Where AI can create meaningful value.
2. **Prioritize** — Which opportunities deserve investment.
3. **Prove** — Build something real, fast enough to matter.

## Embedded execution stages

4. **Deploy** — Into the customer's environment, under their governance.
5. **Scale** — A forward-deployed team executes the roadmap.

## What the first engagement delivers

- An **AI roadmap**
- A working **Proof of Impact** — proof before the big commitment

## Strategy and delivery in parallel

**Track A — Discovery and direction**

1. Understand the business and its priorities
2. Prioritize opportunities worth funding
3. Set architecture and governance
4. Publish the AI roadmap

**Track B — Delivery, in parallel**

1. Pick one or two high-value use cases
2. Build the agent or agentic workflow
3. Deploy inside the customer's own environment
4. Leave something real running in the business

Both tracks converge on a roadmap with a working Proof of Impact so executives decide on evidence from their own environment, not speculation.
